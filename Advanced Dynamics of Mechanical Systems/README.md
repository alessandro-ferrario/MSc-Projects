## Course Overview
This folder contains the dynamic modeling, structural simulations, and experimental parameter identification reports developed for the *Advanced Dynamics of Mechanical Systems* course. The activities focus on modal identification techniques, numerical structural discretization via Finite Element Method (FEM), and vibration mitigation strategies through passive damping devices.

---

## 🔬 Laboratory Activities & Projects

### 1. Experimental Modal Analysis (EMA)
* **Goal:** Extract and identify natural frequencies, mode shapes, and Frequency Response Functions (FRFs) to evaluate the structural dynamics of a cantilever beam and a light rail wheel. The experimental identification procedure is first verified against analytical beam formulations and then extended to the wheel geometry.
* **Methods/Keywords:** Experimental Modal Analysis (EMA), Frequency Response Functions (FRF), Cantilever Beam, Light Rail Wheel, Mode Shapes, Modal Parameter Identification.
* **Software:** MATLAB

### 2. In-Plane Finite Element Modeling & Vibration Control
* **Goal:** Develop an in-plane structural Finite Element Model (FEM) of a truss bridge to evaluate static loading, mode shapes, and resonance conditions. The project investigates system damping matrices, calculates critical traveling load velocities, and includes the design and tuning of a Tuned Mass Damper (TMD) for vibration suppression.
* **Methods/Keywords:** Finite Element Modeling (FEM), Truss Bridge, Structural Dynamics, Tuned Mass Damper (TMD), Critical Velocities, Vibration Suppression.
* **Software:** MATLAB
