# Vehicle Dynamics and Control A - Laboratory Projects

- **Course:** Vehicle Dynamics and Control A | Politecnico di Milano
- **Instructors:** Prof. F. Cheli, Prof. M. Vignati, Dr. M. Belloni
- **Date:** February 2025 - June 2025

## Course Overview
This folder contains the laboratory report developed for the *Vehicle Dynamics and Control A* course. The project focuses on the mathematical modeling and simulation of a vehicle's longitudinal, vertical, and lateral dynamics. It explores the implementation of advanced tire-road contact models and the design of active control systems to optimize stability, performance, and comfort.

---

## 🔬 Laboratory Activities & Projects

### 1. Road-Tire Contact Forces Modelling
* **Goal:** Model the road-tire interaction to analyze the effect of varying parameters on contact forces, with a specific focus on vertical load sensitivity and combined slip conditions during transient behavior.
* **Methods/Keywords:** Pacejka Magic Formula, Longitudinal and Lateral Forces, Combined Slip Effect, Transient Behavior.
* **Software:** MATLAB, Simulink

### 2. Longitudinal Dynamics & Control
* **Goal:** Analyze motor/resistance working points to define maximum speed, tune an optimal front/rear brake pressure distributor, and design a comprehensive Cruise Control system. The control architecture evaluates acceleration/deceleration performance, wind-up effects, and compliance with standard acceleration limits.
* **Methods/Keywords:** Optimal Brake Distribution, Cruise Control, Feedback & Feedforward Control (PI), Anti Wind-Up, Standard Regulations.
* **Software:** MATLAB, Simulink

### 3. Vertical Dynamics & Suspension Systems
* **Goal:** Develop a Quarter Car model to evaluate ride comfort and road holding. The project plots Frequency Response Functions (FRFs) and conflict diagrams to compare the dynamic behavior of passive, semi-active, and fully active suspension systems across different road profiles.
* **Methods/Keywords:** Quarter Car Model, Comfort Indices, Conflict Diagrams, FRFs, Passive/Semi-Active/Active Suspensions.
* **Software:** MATLAB, Simulink

### 4. Lateral Dynamics & Handling Stability
* **Goal:** Evaluate the steady-state and transient lateral behavior of a Single Track model. The study derives the effective axle characteristic curve, analyzes the front-to-rear stiffness ratio, and performs stability analysis using state-space matrix eigenvalues across multiple standardized maneuvers.
* **Methods/Keywords:** Single Track Model, Effective Axle Characteristic Curve, Stability Analysis (Eigenvalues), Steering Step, Sine Dwell, Path Follower, Double Lane Change, Sine Sweep.
* **Software:** MATLAB, Simulink
