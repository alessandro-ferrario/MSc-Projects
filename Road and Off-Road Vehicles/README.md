# Road and Off Road Vehicle Design - Engineering Projects

- **Course:** Road and Off Road Vehicle Design | Politecnico di Milano  
- **Instructors:** Prof. G. Mastinu, Eng. P. Stabile  
- **Academic Year:** 2025 - 2026  

## Course Overview
This folder contains the design reports, kinematic models, structural analyses, and simulations developed for the *Road and Off Road Vehicle Design* course. The course covers different topics regarding the design processes of the automotive industry, including longitudinal and vertical dynamics studies, subsystems implementation, seminars covered by professionists of the sector and a full detalied design project of a McPherson Suspension assembly.

---

## 🔬 Projects, Laboratory Activities & Seminars

### 1. Vehicle Ride Comfort & Vibration Analysis
* **Goal:** Evaluate vertical dynamic behavior and ride comfort across three vehicle architectures using a quarter car model, assessing how mass, spring rate, and damping variations influence body acceleration standard deviation (discomfort index).
* **Methods/Keywords:** Vertical Dynamics, Quarter Car Model, Discomfort Index, Road Holding, Suspension Working Space.
* **Software:** MATLAB

### 2. ICE Vehicle Longitudinal Performance
* **Goal:** Model running resistance and powertrain performance of a baseline sport car under curb and full load conditions, calculating maximum velocity and acceleration times.
* **Methods/Keywords:** Resistance Modeling, Gear Ratio Definition, Longitudinal Dynamics, Grade Resistance, Acceleration Time.
* **Software:** MATLAB

### 3. PHEV Powertrain Simulation & WLTP Cycle Management
* **Goal:** Simulate a Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicle (PHEV) over the WLTP cycle to evaluate fuel consumption, energy efficiency, regenerative braking recapture, and battery State of Charge (SOC) management strategies.
* **Methods/Keywords:** PHEV Powertrain, WLTP Homologation Cycle, Energy Management, State of Charge (SOC), Regenerative Braking.
* **Software:** MATLAB

### 4. Aluminum Wheel Rim Fatigue Life Evaluation
* **Goal:** Perform structural fatigue life prediction for an aluminum alloy wheel rim using finite element modeling under dynamic cornering test standards (rotating bending moment and Sines equivalent stress criterion).
* **Methods/Keywords:** Wheel Fatigue Life, Sines Multiaxial Criterion, Rotating Bending Moment, Dynamic Cornering Test, FEM.
* **Software:** MATLAB

### 5. Racing Motorcycle Braking System Sizing
* **Goal:** Determine hydraulic component sizing and adherence/flip-over deceleration thresholds for a high-performance motorcycle, accounting for high-speed aerodynamic downforce/drag and ergonomic lever force limits.
* **Methods/Keywords:** Motorcycle Braking Limits, Pitchover/Flipping Threshold, Hydraulic Line Sizing, Master Cylinder/Caliper Matching.
* **Software:** Microsoft Excel

### 6. McPherson Suspension Design & Structural Optimization
* **Goal:** Comprehensive design and validation of a McPherson suspension assembly. Combines kinematic optimization (camber curve and steering geometry targets) with topological optimization, FEA structural verification, and formal manufacturing drawings of the lower control arm.
* **Methods/Keywords:** McPherson Kinematics, Lower Control Arm, Topological Optimization, FEM Structural Validation, Technical Drawing.
* **Software:** MATLAB, SolidWorks, Inspire
* **Deliverables:**
  * [Lower Arm Production Drawing (PDF)](./LowerArmDrawing.pdf)
  * [Suspension Assembly Drawing (PDF)](./McPhersonDrawing.pdf)
 
### 7. Dynamic Simulator Ride Perception (DRiSMI)
* **Goal:** Validate quarter car analytical natural frequencies and vibration mode shapes via an active driving simulator session, correlating subjective driver perception with objective cabin telemetry.
* **Methods/Keywords:** Driving Simulator (DRiSMI), Quarter Car Modes, Low/High Frequency Vibration, Haptic Perception.

### 8. Automotive Subsystems & Experimental Testing Facility (LaST Visit)
* **Goal:** Practical examination of critical automotive assemblies and experimental testing rigs at the *Laboratorio sulla Sicurezza dei Trasporti* (LaST - PoliMi). 
* **Methods/Keywords:** MacPherson vs. Multi-Link Kinematics, Carbon-Ceramic Brakes, Active Differential, Clutch Assembly, Experimental Tire Rig (Drum, MoLAS), Pacejka Parameter Identification.

### 9. Wheel Rim Manufacturing & Quality Engineering (Cromodora Seminar)
* **Goal:** Analyze industrial manufacturing methods for aluminum alloy road wheels (low pressure die casting, gravity casting, flow forming, forging) along with APQP and quality compliance pipelines.
* **Methods/Keywords:** Aluminum Casting (LPDC/GDC), Forging, APQP Framework, Wheel Fatigue Standards.

### 10. Automotive Functional Safety (ISO 26262) & D-FMEA (Capgemini Seminars)
* **Goal:** Review systems reliability and functional safety standards across automotive E/E architectures. The modules cover the ISO 26262/IEC 61508 lifecycle, Hazard Analysis and Risk Assessment (HARA), and ASIL classification alongside the execution of the 7-step Design FMEA (D-FMEA) process to identify failure modes, evaluate Risk Priority Numbers (RPN), and define mitigation actions.
* **Methods/Keywords:** Functional Safety (FuSa), ISO 26262, IEC 61508, HARA, ASIL, V-Cycle, Design FMEA (D-FMEA), Risk Priority Number (RPN), Root Cause Analysis.

### 11. Pirelli Vizzola Track Testing: RWD Dynamic Limit Handling
* **Goal:** Field evaluation of rear-wheel-drive vehicle limit handling at the Pirelli Vizzola proving ground. Investigates power-on/off yaw stability, combined friction circle limits, and transient recovery dynamics on low/high friction surfaces.
* **Methods/Keywords:** Friction Circle Limit, RWD Dynamic Handling, Power-On Oversteer, Transient Load Transfer, Proving Ground Testing.
