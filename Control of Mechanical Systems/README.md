## Course Overview
This folder contains the dynamic modeling, state-space formulations, and control design developed for the *Control of Mechanical Systems* course. The project focuses on vehicle longitudinal control, deriving analytical equations of motion under aerodynamic and grade resistance, designing feedback regulators, and analyzing the impact of actuator dynamics and structural drivetrain flexibility on closed-loop stability.

---

## 🔬 Project Overview

### 1. Preliminary Design of a Vehicle Cruise Control System
* **Goal:** Formulate the non-linear equations of motion for a longitudinal vehicle model subject to aerodynamic drag and road slope variations. The system is linearized around steady-state operating conditions to synthesize and tune feedback PI/PID controllers. The study extends to closed-loop stability and bandwidth analysis by integrating electric motor actuator lag and driveline compliance (2-DOF flexible shaft model).
* **Methods/Keywords:** Longitudinal Dynamics, Linearization, PI/PID Tuning, Root Locus, Driveline Compliance (2-DOF), Actuator Dynamics, Stability Margins.
* **Software:** MATLAB, Simulink
